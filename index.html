What we do

These are examples, not a menu. If it’s an engineering problem, we want to hear about it.

Process engineering & design

From concept to FEED: design basis, PFDs and P&IDs, heat and mass balances, equipment sizing and datasheets.

Simulation, CFD & optimisation

Aspen HYSYS and Aspen Plus models, hydraulics, sensitivity studies and debottlenecking, and CFD for mixing, heat transfer and flow.

Process safety

HAZID and HAZOP support, LOPA, relief and flare studies to API 520 and 521, with actions that actually close.

Asset integrity, RBI & third‑party inspection

Risk‑based inspection planning, integrity reviews and independent third‑party inspection for plant that has to keep running.

Plant performance & energy efficiency

Troubleshooting, capacity studies, waste heat recovery and energy efficiency — to get more from the plant you already have.

Engineering software

Calculation tools and automation, built because the engineering needed them. Starting with Fugacity, our process simulation tool — a free licence for every school and university, forever; support is optional and paid.

How we’re set up

A small team. We lead the work, set the scope and stay responsible for quality. When a job needs more people, we bring in vetted specialists and review everything before it reaches you.

Every project follows one method

DMAIC, the Lean Six Sigma way of fixing a problem with data: Define, Measure, Analyse, Improve, Control. Led by a Six Sigma Green Belt.
